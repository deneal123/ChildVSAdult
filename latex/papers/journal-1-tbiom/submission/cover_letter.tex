Dear Editor,

\textbf{Working draft, not submission-ready.} The institutional determination, access route,
full-method comparisons, complete strong-backbone matrix and human adjudication remain pending.
Before this letter is used for a substantially revised new submission, its factual statements
must be updated from the final evidence and every \pending{field} resolved.

\vspace{3mm}
\noindent\textbf{1. Human-participant review and consent.}
The study retrospectively processes face images and captions from public social-media ``then/now''
posts. The corpus includes images depicting minors, biometric templates are computed for research,
and individual consent was not obtained. No interaction, intervention, deployment or automated
decision about a person occurs. No formal institutional determination has yet been obtained.
The final letter must name \pending{official committee name},
\pending{decision / determination wording}, \pending{date}, and \pending{number}. The study,
manuscript and release package must follow the scope and conditions of that determination; specifically,
\pending{state whether retrospective data, minors, annotation, controlled access and publication of
examples are covered}. Any category outside that scope must be excluded and all dependent results
recomputed before submission. The actual determination and point-by-point compliance mapping must
accompany the submission; this draft does not assert that those actions have occurred.

\vspace{3mm}
\noindent\textbf{2. Dataset availability and verification.}
The social-media images, captions, original identifiers, biometric templates and corpus-derived model
weights are not publicly released. Public posting is not treated as permission to assemble and
redistribute a longitudinal biometric database. Subject to the committee determination above and
written confirmation from the journal, we propose \pending{approved controlled-access arrangement},
governed by a data-use agreement, identity verification, purpose limitation, secure processing and a
documented deletion procedure. If the journal does not accept that route, claims depending on data
that cannot be independently examined will be removed rather than described as reproducible.

The public submission package contains only non-sensitive source code, configurations, aggregate
machine-readable results and provenance manifests; row-level protocols and biometric derivatives
are withheld. Final package disclosure, licenses and artifact availability must be verified before
asserting that it contains no prohibited data or that evaluations are independently reproducible.
External data alone do not make private-corpus-trained checkpoints available. Three-seed bidirectional transfer to an independent
non-VK source is reported as cross-platform evidence, not as cross-cultural or universal validation.

\vspace{3mm}
\noindent\textbf{3. Previous T-BIOM submission and substantial changes.}
An earlier version was submitted to T-BIOM as manuscript \texttt{TBIOM-2026-07-0222} and received a
final rejection. This is a new submission, not a revision under that record. The earlier manuscript
was not published. Before submission, verify that no substantially overlapping work remains under
review elsewhere and update the publication-status disclosure. Verified changes and remaining gates are:

\begin{itemize}
\item the earlier author-only ethics rationale is replaced by explicit pending institutional/legal
      gates and a draft conditional governance/access plan; the actual determination is still required;
\item canonical CACD-VS 10-fold verification and three-seed bidirectional cross-platform tests
      broaden evaluation, but the small Reddit training source does not establish population-wide transfer;
\item restricted common-protocol MTLFace/CACon-CP comparators are separated from a published-number
      literature table; complete, equal-budget three-seed full-method reproduction remains pending;
\item a planned three-seed learning-rate $\times$ trainable-scope $\times$ negative-type AdaFace IR-101
      study has partial accepted loss, gradient, CKA, drift, separability and age-probe evidence,
      not a completed matrix or a proven ceiling/plasticity/forgetting mechanism;
\item the data funnel, retained/rejected quality distributions, independent embedding sensitivity,
      and pretraining-overlap limitation are documented with manifests; a held-out $5\times400$
      blinded-audit pack and scorer are prepared, and the final submission will incorporate both
      independent response files and adjudication rather than illustrative values;
\item the LFW alignment defect in the earlier analysis was corrected, and every claim now states the
      observed benchmark-dependent trade-off rather than a no-cost gain.
\end{itemize}

An internal point-by-point matrix maps every Co-EIC, AE and reviewer comment to the change, evidence
artifact and manuscript location; we will upload it if the new-submission portal provides an
