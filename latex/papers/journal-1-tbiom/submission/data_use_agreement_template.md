## Recipient obligations

The recipient will:

1. use the data only for the approved non-commercial reproducibility purpose;
2. restrict access to the named researchers on institution-managed encrypted
   systems protected by MFA and access logging;
3. not identify or contact a person, link records to another dataset, recover
   platform identifiers, search for source posts, or attempt re-identification;
4. not use the data for surveillance, ranking, eligibility, employment, insurance,
   policing, border control, or any automated decision about a person;
5. not transfer, publish, sublicense, expose through an API, or upload the data or
   biometric derivatives to a third party, cloud model service, or public model hub;
6. not train or distribute an operational face-identification model from the data;
7. publish only aggregate outputs that pass the provider's disclosure review; no
   image, caption, identifier, embedding, nearest-neighbour example, or row-level
   result may be published without separate written permission;
8. notify the provider within 24 hours of suspected loss, access, disclosure, or
   prohibited use and suspend processing during investigation;
9. apply revocation notices promptly and preserve no working copy of revoked items;
10. on expiry or request, stop processing and delete all copies and derived
    biometric templates within 30 days, subject only to documented immutable-backup
    rotation, and return a signed deletion certificate.

## Provider obligations and limits

The provider will supply only the committee-approved minimum package, a manifest,
and a secure channel; maintain an access/revocation ledger; and notify active
recipients of material corrections or withdrawals. Access conveys no ownership or
copyright licence beyond the limited research use above. The data are supplied
without a fitness or accuracy warranty. Nothing in this agreement overrides
applicable law, data-subject rights, platform terms, or the ethics decision.

## Breach, termination, and publication

The provider may suspend access immediately for a security risk, participant
withdrawal, legal/committee instruction, or suspected breach. A material breach
terminates access and may be reported to the parties' institutions and competent
authorities. Manuscripts using the data must cite the dataset version and access
route, describe access limitations, and must not imply that controlled access makes
the underlying faces anonymous.
